See shotfleet work

First, a few short clips of the commands. Below them is my list of what I have actually run shotfleet on, with dates, and what happened.

Clips

The clips are real terminal recordings from one session on the fixture apps in shotfleet's own repository; titles, subtitles and the sped-up labels were added afterwards.

Evidence

What I ran, when, and what came out. I only list runs that are written down. The note under each line names the file in my private repository where I keep the record (run logs, check.json). Email hello@shotfleet.com and I'll send you the one you want to see.

Built to run on macOS 13 or later on Apple silicon (the build declares 13.0 as its minimum), but I haven't run it on macOS 13. My full runs, with simulators and emulators, were all on macOS 27. On macOS 14 and 15 I ran the installer, doctor and check for version 0.1.6, and on macOS 26.2 in a virtual machine for 0.1.2. Other macOS versions are not tested yet.

Releases

  • 2026-10-11, release 0.1.9. The signed, notarized build passed 65 of 65 assertions on iPhone and iPad simulators and an Android emulator in 31 minutes 37 seconds, on Maestro 2.0.10 with iOS pinned to the 26.4 runtime. It took three tries. The first said it had passed, but 16 of its Android assertions never ran, because my script had Python 3.9 on its path and the helper that runs them needs 3.11 or newer. That was my mistake. The second failed: the four iOS simulators stopped answering while my Mac was short of memory, and 19 assertions failed. Neither one counts. The third passed, with the Android assertions included. The same fixture app also ran on Maestro 2.11.0 in the first job: Android passed 30 of 30 in 2 minutes and iOS 36 of 36 in 15 minutes 45 seconds. Record: docs/compat.md, research/019-gate-2026-10-11/
  • 2026-10-11, the new 0.1.9 stop, on the shipped build. On a made-up React Native app with no translation files, check exited 2 and asked for --strings, and init exited 2 where 0.1.8 had exited 0. run stopped at "Maestro isn't installed" on my Mac before it got to the strings check, so that part is proven by the unit tests only. No device was used. Record: research/019-gate-2026-10-11/proofs.out
  • 2026-10-08, release 0.1.8. The signed, notarized build passed 65 of 65 assertions on iPhone and iPad simulators and an Android emulator in 24 minutes 55 seconds, on Maestro 2.0.10 with iOS pinned to the 26.4 runtime. Then the same fixture app ran on Maestro 2.11.0: Android passed 30 of 30 in 1 minute 13 seconds and iOS 36 of 36 in 16 minutes 40 seconds. My Mac's swap was over 90 percent full the whole time, so these are slow-Mac timings. Record: docs/compat.md, research/018-gate-2026-10-08/
  • 2026-10-08, the new 0.1.8 changes, from source. On the fixture app with Maestro 2.0.10 and iOS 26.4: a plain-text scrollUntilVisible was translated, every flow had the settle step in front of each screenshot on iOS and Android, zh-Hans was used as zh-CN on an Android build that ships zh-CN, and check --app on the two wger Flutter builds asked for --strings and then checked 12 and 18 screenshots with 0 problems. A missing iPad set in a parent folder showed as missing with exit code 1, and [expect] with key passed on the right screen and failed on the wrong one. The regex warning, the port 7001 message, the splash message and the kept failure screenshot were tested with fakes only, not on a device. The Mac was busy, so the busy-Mac warning showed up. Record: research/018-device-check-2026-10-08/
  • 2026-10-08, release 0.1.7. The signed, notarized build passed 65 of 65 assertions on iPhone and iPad simulators and an Android emulator in 35 minutes 50 seconds, on Maestro 2.0.10 with iOS pinned to the 26.4 runtime. Record: docs/compat.md
  • 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-08, the new 0.1.7 commands. On the fixture app, German and Japanese, iPhone and iPad: run skipped the iOS 27.0 simulators and said so, did not retry a flow that failed, and run --retry-failed captured only the failed language. check printed the coverage table and --compare, and export --print-upload printed the fastlane command. The Mac was busy, so the busy-Mac warning showed up. Maestro 2.0.10 only. Record: research/017-device-check-2026-10-07/
  • 2026-10-08, the live 0.1.6. On GitHub's hosted Apple silicon runners with macOS 14.8.9 and 15.7.9, the installer, shotfleet --version, doctor and check ran again, this time on the build customers get now. No simulator or emulator was run there. Record: research/macos-14-15-live-0.1.6-2026-10-08.md
  • 2026-10-06, release 0.1.4. The same signed build passed 29 of 29 assertions on an Android emulator and 36 of 36 on iPhone and iPad simulators. The first iOS try ran out of memory on my Mac, so iOS passed in a second run a few hours later. Record: docs/handoff.md section 17, STATUS.md
  • 2026-10-06, release 0.1.3. The same signed build passed 36 of 36 assertions on iPhone and iPad simulators and 29 of 29 on an Android emulator, in two separate runs. My Mac didn't have enough memory to get one full run with all three through. Record: docs/handoff.md section 17, docs/compat.md, STATUS.md
  • 2026-10-05, release 0.1.3. On Android, shotfleet reads the screen text from Maestro's UI hierarchy instead of from pixels. On the Android fixture app it passed 30 of 30 assertions with Maestro 2.11.0, and 29 of 29 with Maestro 2.0.10, where it still reads pixels. Record: docs/compat.md and the Android fixture-app run log
  • 2026-10-05, release 0.1.2. The signed build ran the fixture app on iPhone and iPad simulators and an Android emulator and passed 65 of 65 assertions in 37 minutes. Record: research/0.1.2-plan.md (Outcome), docs/handoff.md section 15, docs/compat.md
  • 2026-10-05, same run. In the iPad part the Mac was heavily loaded and 3 of 4 simulators stopped answering; shotfleet finished all 4 languages on the 1 simulator left, with 0 problems. Record: docs/handoff.md section 15, research/0.1.2-plan.md
  • 2026-10-05, release 0.1.1. On GitHub's hosted Apple silicon runners with macOS 14.8.9 and 15.7.9, the installer, shotfleet --version, doctor and check all ran; no simulator or emulator was run there. Record: research/limits-platform-reach-2026-10-05.md, docs/compat.md
