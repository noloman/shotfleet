The licence

  • One licence is for one developer. Use it on up to three of your own Macs and in your CI.
  • Unlimited apps and projects, including client and commercial work.
  • shotfleet check is free for everyone, and run is free for up to 2 languages. The licence unlocks every language and export.
  • shotfleet needs a Mac with Apple silicon for now. There is no Intel build yet.
  • Don't share your key or publish it. Moving to a new Mac: shotfleet deactivate on the old one.

Updates

A year of updates is included. After that, the version you have keeps working for as long as macOS, Xcode and Maestro let it, and as long as the licence check can run: it asks Polar about once a week, and works offline for up to 30 days. I plan to offer an optional renewal for further updates after the first year; it is not available yet.

Refunds

If shotfleet doesn't work for your app, ask for a refund within 14 days of buying and you'll get it, no questions asked. Email manulorenzop@gmail.com with your order email.

Payment

You buy through Polar, which sells shotfleet to you as the merchant of record. Polar handles the payment, applies sales tax or VAT under its own terms, and sends your receipt and licence key. I never see your card details.

No warranty

shotfleet is provided as it is. I fix bugs as fast as I can, but I can't promise it will work with every app, every Xcode or every emulator. If something goes wrong, my liability is limited to what you paid.
