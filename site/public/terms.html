License

shotfleet is free and open source.

Plain terms, no surprises. If anything here isn't clear, email me.

The license

  • shotfleet is released under the MIT license. You can use it, copy it, change it and share it, including for client and commercial work. Keep the license notice when you redistribute it.
  • Every command and every language is free: doctor, init, run, check and export. There is no key, no account and no license server.
  • Unlimited apps, projects and Macs. CI works like on a Mac.
  • shotfleet needs a Mac with Apple silicon for now. There's no Intel build yet.

Sponsoring

shotfleet is built and maintained by one person. If it saves you time, you can sponsor it on GitHub Sponsors. Sponsoring is optional, it unlocks nothing, and it doesn't change what you can do with shotfleet.

Earlier versions

Versions up to 0.1.10 were sold with a license key that unlocked every language and export. If you bought one, thank you. From the first free release on, shotfleet no longer checks a key, so nothing is left to renew or activate. Every version stays downloadable at shotfleet.com/download/<version>/shotfleet-macos-arm64.tar.gz. Questions about an old purchase: hello@shotfleet.com.

No warranty

shotfleet is provided as it is. I fix bugs as fast as I can, but I can't promise it will work with every app, every Xcode or every emulator. If something goes wrong, my liability is limited as the MIT license says.
