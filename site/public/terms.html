Licence

What you get when you buy shotfleet.

Plain terms, no surprises. If anything here isn't clear, email me.

The licence

  • One licence is for one developer. The key has 3 activations, for your own Macs, and your CI uses none.
  • Unlimited apps and projects, including client and commercial work.
  • shotfleet check is free for everyone, and run is free for up to 2 languages. The licence unlocks every language and export. doctor and init are free too, and the 2 free languages work on both platforms and every device size.
  • shotfleet needs a Mac with Apple silicon for now. There's no Intel build yet.
  • Don't share your key or publish it. A key can be activated 3 times. Activating again on a Mac that still has the key saved counts nothing. After shotfleet deactivate, a new macOS install or a new user account, the same Mac counts again. shotfleet deactivate removes the key from a Mac, but Gumroad can't give the activation back from there: if you run out, email hello@shotfleet.com and I'll reset the count.

Updates

A year of updates is included. After that, the version you have keeps working for as long as macOS, Xcode and Maestro let it, and as long as the licence check can run: it asks Gumroad about once a week, and works offline for up to 30 days (a CI key, SHOTFLEET_KEY, is checked online on every run and has no offline limit). I plan to offer an optional renewal for further updates after the first year; it is not available yet.

Refunds

You can ask for a refund within 14 days of buying and you'll get it, no questions asked, and you don't have to say why. Email hello@shotfleet.com with your order email.

A refund ends the licence. The key stops unlocking every language and export the next time shotfleet checks it with Gumroad, which happens about once a week (up to 30 days later if the Mac is offline); a CI key is checked on every run, so it stops as soon as a run reaches Gumroad. check and the 2 free languages keep working, and nothing you already made is deleted.

Payment

You buy through Gumroad, which sells shotfleet to you as the merchant of record. Gumroad handles the payment, collects and pays sales tax or VAT, and sends your receipt and licence key. Prices are before VAT or sales tax, which Gumroad adds at checkout where it applies, so a buyer in the EU pays the price plus VAT; a business with a VAT ID can enter it at checkout. I never see your card details. Refunds go through Gumroad too: the money, including any VAT, goes back to the way you paid, and a card refund usually takes 5 to 10 business days to show up. Gumroad may also refund a purchase on its own in the first 90 days, and buyers in Brazil have a 7-day right of withdrawal.

No warranty

shotfleet is provided as it is. I fix bugs as fast as I can, but I can't promise it will work with every app, every Xcode or every emulator. If something goes wrong, my liability is limited to what you paid.
