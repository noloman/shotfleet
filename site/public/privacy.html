Privacy

This page has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. The only thing it stores is what you send when you join the waitlist.

What I store when you join

  • Your email address.
  • Which stores you said you ship on (iOS, Android or both).
  • Your country, which Cloudflare works out from your IP address. The IP address itself isn't stored.
  • The site that linked you here and any utm_ tags in the link, so I can tell which post people came from.
  • The date and time you joined.

What I use it for

To send you one email when shotfleet is available to buy, and to count how many people are interested. I don't share it, sell it, or add you to any other list.

Where it's kept, and for how long

In Cloudflare Workers KV, Cloudflare's storage for the server that runs this page. Cloudflare also processes your IP address to serve the page and to limit how often the form can be sent. I delete the list 30 days after the launch email goes out, or sooner if you ask.
