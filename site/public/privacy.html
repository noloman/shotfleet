Privacy

What I keep, and how to delete it.

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts, and it loads nothing from other servers. The only thing it stores is what you send when you join the waitlist or write to me.

When you join the waitlist

  • Your email address.
  • Which stores you said you ship on.
  • Your country, which Cloudflare works out from your IP address. The IP address itself isn't stored.
  • The site that linked you here and any utm_ tags in the link, so I know which post people came from.
  • The date and time you joined.

What I use it for

To send you one email when shotfleet is available to buy, and to count how many people are interested. I don't share it, sell it, or add you to any other list.

Where it's kept, and for how long

In Cloudflare Workers KV, the storage for the small server behind this page. Cloudflare also processes your IP address to serve the page and to limit how often the forms can be sent. I delete the list 30 days after the launch email goes out, or sooner if you ask.
