## Findings for shotfleet

1. `check --app <APK>` works on a Compose Multiplatform APK (the `.cvr` assets are read); the Flutter APK of the wger job could not use it. Useful to say in docs.
2. The ambiguity warnings (`'Bookmarks' also means 'Bookmark'` and so on) are noise here: the taps were right in every language. A tap on a bottom-bar label matched the label, not the empty-state text, because the labels are exact full-string matches.
3. Android check evidence is OCR only (Maestro 2.0.10); the run header says so, the check JSON says "ocr", but `check` cannot catch live content or a wrong-but-translated screen. All 35 frames needed a human look.
4. Run 2's log printed `another shotfleet is preparing macOS text recognition; waiting`: another session's shotfleet was warming up the recogniser at the same time (not mine).
5. The chain's naming (`out-<name>`) is my script's; the shotfleet side is fine: `out = "out-android"` is renamed by hand after each run.
